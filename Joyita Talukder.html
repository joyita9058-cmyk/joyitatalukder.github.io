High-Performance Adsorption of Methyl Orange by a Chitosan/PVA/CuO Nanocomposite Membrane

Undergraduate Thesis  ·  Supervisor: Dr. Md. Muktadir Billah, Dept. of MME, BUET

ZnO & Ga-ZnO Colloidal Quantum Dots for Thin-Film Optoelectronics
2024 — 2025
Solid State Physics Laboratory  ·  Dept. of MME, BUET  ·  Supervisor: Dr. Md. Azizar Rahman

Pioneered solution-phase growth of Zinc Oxide (ZnO) colloidal quantum dots and gallium-doped variants (Ga-ZnO) in BUET's Solid State Physics Laboratory. Engineered uniform thin films via precision spin-coating for optoelectronic device architectures and luminescent down-shifter components.

  • Explored applications in luminescent down-shifter architectures for photovoltaic and LED systems.
Erbium-Doped MnFe₂O₄ Spinel Nanoparticles for Supercapacitor Energy Storage
February 2026 — Ongoing
Ongoing Research  ·  BCSIR  ·  Authors: Joyita Talukder, Wasif ur Rahman  ·  Supervisor: Aninda Nafiz Sir

Investigating how erbium (Er) incorporation into the MnFe₂O₄ (MFO) spinel lattice influences its redox chemistry, charge-transfer kinetics, ion accessibility, and electrochemical energy-storage performance for supercapacitor applications. MnFe₂O₄ is selected for its rich Mn/Fe redox chemistry, low cost, and tunable spinel structure; Er doping is expected to create lattice distortion, modify Mn/Fe oxidation states, and improve charge-transfer kinetics.

  • Structural Engineering — Does Er incorporation modify MFO spinel lattice parameters and cation distribution? (XRD, Raman)
  • Electronic Modification — Does Er change Mn/Fe oxidation state distribution and defect chemistry? (XPS, FTIR)
  • Electrochemical Kinetics — Does Er improve electron transfer rate and ion accessibility in KOH electrolyte? (EIS, CV, Mott-Schottky)
  • Charge-Storage Mechanism — Is charge storage surface-controlled or diffusion-controlled across Er compositions?
  • Device Development — Can the optimum Er-MFO composition power a stable asymmetric supercapacitor?

Millsorpt — Mill Scale to Metal Free
2025
Materials Symposium 2025  ·  BUET, Bangladesh  ·  Capstone Project (Group)
Innovative conversion of ferrous mill scale (steel manufacturing waste) into effective heavy-metal adsorbents for water purification — a triple waste treatment approach. Mill scale treated with H₂SO₄, precipitated with NaOH, and dried to yield hematite (Fe₂O₃) as the final adsorbent product. Performance characterised by AAS and UV-Vis spectroscopy; XRD confirmed phase composition.
Poster presented at Materials Symposium 2025  ·  Dept. of MME, BUET  ·  Submitted to Dr. Ahmed Sharif, Dr. Fahmida Gulshan, Dr. H. M. Mamun Al Rashed
